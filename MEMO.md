# To Ritu Deshpande: Returns-risk pilot decision

**Recommendation:** Pilot targeted confirmation calls while preserving dispatch promises. Do not automatically hold orders or promise 95% accuracy. The working tool provides risk scores and readable model factors without a paid API.

**The number.** We found 1,200 returns among 10,504 distinct historical orders (11.42%). Simply predicting “no return” already produces 88.58% historical accuracy while identifying no returns. Our revised model separates risk better than that baseline: on April–June orders its ranking score was 0.755, where 0.5 represents chance. This is not 75.5% accuracy. That period had already been examined during development, so its revised-model results are diagnostic, not a fresh independent test. We cannot claim 95% accuracy or proven future performance.

**The rupees.** Finance's policy gives Rs 1,150 per return and Rs 45 per completed confirmation call. If calls prevent 35% of otherwise-occurring returns, the break-even risk is about 11.18%. In a retrospective scenario restricted to supported inputs, 645 calls cost Rs 29,025. The selected group contained 148 historical returns. Applying the pilot effect yields about 51.8 prevented returns, Rs 59,570 in avoided handling costs, and **Rs 30,545 net handling-cost savings** across the 2,126-order evaluation period. These are assumptions applied to history, not observed savings. This queue breaks even at roughly 17.05% prevention. Overhead, unsuccessful attempts, lost margin, complaints and lifetime value are not fully captured.

**Why no automatic holds.** The policy reports 12% cancellation among orders held beyond 24 hours, but does not establish incremental harm, margin lost or return prevention from holding. Shield customers also have valuable repeat business and free-return entitlements. Call effectiveness cannot be assumed to equal hold effectiveness.

**Next week.** Confirm return observation windows, contact capacity and contribution margins. Freeze the model and calling policy. Start a customer-randomized pilot among eligible flagged orders, comparing helpful confirmation calls with usual service without delaying dispatch. Track attempted/completed calls, returns, cancellations, costs and complaints, separately by channel and current Shield status. Wait for complete outcome windows before deciding whether to expand.

**Limits.** The service routes more than ten earlier orders to manual review. Other inputs outside observed ranges can display explicitly provisional estimates, per the requested demo behavior; their financial benefit is not estimated. The threshold of ten is an operating preference, not a proven accuracy boundary. Restrict the proposed pilot to supported inputs. Fresh labelled outcomes are needed to validate the revised model and intervention.
